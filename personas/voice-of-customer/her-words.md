## 4. Anti-language: phrases to avoid
| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| "Losing your hair on Ozempic?" / any "you" + her medication or health state | Meta personal-attributes rule (claims guide) |
| "Ozempic hair loss", drug brand names in ad copy | trademark + implied affiliation; prefer "the shot", "GLP-1" |
| "reverses", "stops", "cures", "treats", "prevents" (hair loss, TE, alopecia) | drug claims (claims guide hard ban #2) |
| "clinically proven" | not true for our finished product; also the exact phrase she distrusts (research §4.7 monologue and §7.5, *Stated*) |
| "safe with your GLP-1" | hard ban #1. Say "check with your doctor about your routine" |
| "first / only GLP-1 hair supplement" | false; hard ban #4 |
| "just eat more protein", "it's just the weight loss", "it's just TE" | the advice she is sick of; she bounces (research §4.9 P1). Only voice it to agree-then-add |
| "rapid weight loss" said as *our* explanation | P5 rejects it ("20 lbs in a year is NOT rapid") |
| "the real cause is DHT" stated as fact | load-bearing caveat; education, not proven cause |
| weight numbers ("lost 100 lbs"), "skinny", "fat", body before/afters | Meta weight-loss / body-image rules |
| "regrow 200%", hair-count percentages | treatment-style promise; her floor is "make it stop" anyway |
| "buried study", "doctors don't want you to know" | the hype posture of the competitor we position against (research §7.3) |
| "quit", "pause", "instead of your shot" | P2 bounces instantly (research §4.9) |
| "bald" in our voice | fine in her mouth; in ours it can read as fear-mongering (*Inferred*, test) |
