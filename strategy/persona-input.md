**Why this split (Inferred):** P1 has the strongest voice and is the woman every rival leaves stranded with "eat more
protein". P2 is big (research ~25%, *Stated*) but may not buy; two concepts are enough to find out. P6 is the owner's
bet on white space with almost no evidence; one cheap concept tests whether Meta can even find her.

## 2. Proposed persona decisions (owner to approve)
1. **P4 (perimenopause + the shot) becomes an overlay**, not a separate audience: a "late 40s / 50s" line or a woman
   45–58 on screen sits on top of P1 or P2 ads. Reason: her trigger and angle match P1/P3 with one extra fact
   (`personas.md` part 2). (*Inferred*)
2. **P5 (slow loser, often diabetic) becomes proof inside P1 ads**, never an audience. Diabetes is a health condition:
   never name or target it. Her best line ("My weightloss has been anything but rapid...", §5.4b · r/Ozempic) can be a
   supporting beat. (*Inferred*)
3. **P3 (the mechanism believer) gets no dedicated concept in round 1.** She is likely to buy from the P1 mechanism ads
   anyway (research: "highest intent", *Stated*). Revisit if comments show her showing up.
4. **P7 (quitter's regret): retargeting only**, no cold spend (research ~2%, *Stated*).

## 4. Who the ads will probably reach (Data-limited)

(menopause, stress). That is fine for reach but means **CPA by persona can only be read from the ad name, comments and a
post-purchase survey**. Until the survey exists, "P1 won" really means "the P1-written ad won".
