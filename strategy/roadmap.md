# Roadmap: GLP-1 hair, first quarter of testing

> **Status: Proposed — owner approval pending.** Nothing here is decided until the owner approves it in
> `running-notes/decisions.md`.

## Diagnosis
**Every rival is telling her the shedding is nutrition (or that she "doesn't have to choose"), but the woman we want has
already fixed her protein and is still shedding; the open ground is the honest "why" (agree that weight loss plays a
part, then explain the estrogen → DHT layer, with the doses on the label), and we have weeks, not months, before Bloom &
Bond's hype version of the same story fills it.**

What this rules out: any idea that leads with nutrition, with permission alone, with numbers we can't source, with a
promise to stop or reverse anything, or that needs a testimonial we don't have.

Evidence behind the diagnosis: MDhair, Wellbel and Nutrafol women's GLP-1 ads are nutrition- or permission-led
(Verified, Trendtrack/Atria 2026-11-16); 8 of 114 quotes say "did it all, still shedding" (§5.5, Stated); Bloom & Bond
started the estrogen → DHT story with "83%" and "14 times" claims 8 days ago (Verified, Atria, 1 ad).

## The 3 priorities (ranked)

### Priority 1 · P1 "did everything right" · territory: the honest why (agree, then add)
- **Angles:** A01 (protein was never the whole story), A16, A05, A07, A31 (guilt removal + new cause), A22 (bodyguard
  analogy), A29 (the honest version: doses on the label, no hype) as the proof layer.
- **Why now:** the nutrition story is everywhere, so her failed-fix frustration has no home; Balmbare (who had this
  story) shows 0 of 19 active hair ads (Verified), and Bloom & Bond is moving in loudly.
- **Evidence band:** **HIGH** for her voice (8 of 114 quotes across several subreddits); **MEDIUM** for the gap (our
  competitor read is one day of Trendtrack/Atria data). Overall: HIGH-leaning MEDIUM.
- **What would prove it:** at least one P1 concept reaches CPA at or below target **and** ROAS at or above break-even at
  meaningful spend (about 2× target CPA per concept, `.claude/skills/log-test`), with hook rate ≥ 30% and CTR around 1%+
  (`playbook/metrics.md` starting benchmarks).
- **What would kill it:** all three P1 concepts get good hook rates but CTR and CPA both fail at meaningful spend →
  the story stops her but doesn't sell cold. Then move the mechanism to the advertorial/retargeting and test a simpler
  cold opener. If hook rates are also low, the opener (not the angle) is wrong: iterate the hook once before killing.

### Priority 2 · P2 "not quitting the shot" · territory: permission *with a reason* ("isn't vanity" + why)
- **Angles:** A03 (isn't vanity), A09, A21 (the shed restarts at every dose step-up, P2 version), A33 (double life
  stage) as a later overlay.
- **Why now:** "you don't have to choose" has become the category default (MDhair, Wellbel, Verified), so P2 ads that
  stop at permission will be invisible; the guilt of caring ("isn't vanity") and the dose step-up are unclaimed.
- **Evidence band:** **MEDIUM**: HIGH for the ache ("I love the weight loss... but man, I miss my hair", §5.3), LOW for
  urgency (6 of the 10 trade-off quotes sound settled, §5.3b). Dose step-up: 5 of 114 quotes.
- **What would prove it:** a P2 concept matches the P1 concepts on CPA and ROAS → she is a buyer, not only a feeler.
- **What would kill it:** high hook and hold rates but CTR and conversion clearly below the P1 concepts at meaningful
  spend → P2 "accepters" watch and don't buy. Then P2 becomes a support line inside P1 ads and a retargeting message.

### Priority 3 · P6 "the preventer" + "don't wait and hope" · territory: the undecided woman (against do-nothing)
- **Angles:** A30 (don't wait and hope), A20 (prevention, research-deferred) only in its compliant form.
- **Why now:** in our 20-hook sample almost nobody speaks to the unaware woman (Verified, `hooks-swipe.md`), and
  "do nothing / wait and see" is the biggest real competitor (research §7.1, Stated).
- **Evidence band:** **LOW**: 0 quotes from women before they start; 1 quote "I hate that I have to 'wait and see'"
  (§5.4 · r/Wegovy); the lifetime-value claim is unproven. The research itself defers prevention to a second wave
  (§9.5). One cheap concept only.
- **What would prove it:** CTR near 1% at CPA within target, and comments that show pre-shed or early-shed women.
- **What would kill it:** CTR well below the other concepts and no purchases at meaningful spend, or comments that
  show only women already deep in the shed (then it is just another P1 ad).

## Roads not taken (kill list)
Ideas that clash with these are cut at evaluation, however good they sound.
1. **Leading with "you don't have to choose" alone.** Crowded (MDhair, Wellbel). Allowed only as a payoff after a reason.
2. **Leading with nutrition** ("your hair is last in line", "eat more protein", "it's the rapid change"). Their story.
3. **Hype stats and news framing:** "breaking news", "14 times", "83% of women", any number we can't source, "doctors
   don't want you to know", "buried study".
4. **Attacking minoxidil's efficacy** (hard ban 5). A06 as written is cut. Her dependency fear may be voiced in her
   words only, after a compliance check.
5. **Naming Nutrafol (or any rival) aggressively** (A08, "$88 a month on Nutrafol and it did nothing"). Transparency
   makes the point without names.
6. **"The real cause is DHT" as a fact**, or "it was never a nutrition problem" said flatly. Always agree first; "one
   reason", "can".
7. **Treat / stop / reverse / prevent / cure**, "clinically proven", "first / only", "safe with your shot".
8. **"You" + her medication or health state** ("Losing hair on the shot?", "You're not quitting the shot",
   "the week you upped your dose"). Rewrite in first or third person.
9. **Permanence fear** ("only permanent if you wait", A15) as a lead. Too close to the load-bearing caveat.
10. **Results from anyone**: invented testimonials, AI characters who say it worked, first-person timelines ("Week 3:
    less in the drain").
11. **Zoomed scalp, hair or body before/afters, weight numbers.**
12. **Drug brand names in ad copy** (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, "Ozempic face") until the owner sets the risk level.
13. **Cold spend on P7 or on P5 as an audience** (diabetes must never be named or targeted).
14. **The shower-drain clump as the lead visual** of new concepts (saturated); one control variation at most.

## Testing mix
- The owner's working rule is **70% iterations on winners / 20% new angles / 10% wild**.
- **There are no winners yet** (no real ad data; the weekly report has only run on SAMPLE data). So the first rounds
  can't be 70% iterations. **Round 1 proposal: 5 of 6 concepts are new core angles (about 85%), 1 is the wild slot
  (about 15%)**, spread P1 3 · P2 2 · P6 1. The wild slot is concept 6 (an animated song for P6), which is also the
  priority-3 test, so its result tells us less (two new things at once). Said plainly so nobody over-reads it.
- **Round 2:** iterate whatever clears the bar (hook, then format, then persona; one variable each, 3 iterations),
  move toward 50 / 35 / 15, then 70 / 20 / 10 once 2–3 winners exist.
- About half of new concepts aimed at unaware/problem-aware buyers (method §2): round 1 has 4 of 6.
