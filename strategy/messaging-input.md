## 4. Word rules for every brief (from `her-words.md` and the claims guide)
- Use: shedding, ponytail, hair tie, clip, "the shot" / "weekly shot" / "weight-loss journey", "supports fuller,
  healthier-looking hair", "one reason", "can".
- Never: "you" + her medication or health state, drug brand names in copy (owner decision pending), treat / cure /
  reverse / stop / prevent, clinically proven, first / only, "the real cause is DHT", weight numbers, "bald" in our voice.
