# Product input: what to lead with, and what to be honest about

## 1. The facts we can say (Stated: label or founder)
- 2,000 mg cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil + 200 mg saw palmetto per serving; **2 softgels a day**; 60 softgels = 30 days.
- **90-day money-back guarantee** (exact mechanics not finalised by the founder, §8.3).
- Offers: B1G1 $39.95 (1 month, default) · B2G2 $79.90 · B3G3 $119.85 (3 months + free guide). Subscription % unknown.
- cGMP facility, third-party tested (founder). "45,000+ happy customers" is a site badge, **not** GLP-1-specific: don't
  use it in GLP-1 ads without the owner's OK.

## 2. What to lead with, in this order (Inferred, from research §7.5 and §6.6)

| 4 | **The 3-month bundle** | The default 1-month offer sells a third of a fair trial, and while weight is still coming off the trigger keeps firing (§1.3) | "Three months is what a fair trial needs." **Bloom & Bond note (Verified, Atria):** it runs the same two ingredients, the same story and a 90-day guarantee, and
says "The only brand with both". So **formula + guarantee are no longer unique.** What stays ours is the *honest
posture*: no "breaking news", no unsourced percentages, no "my doctor put me on". Lead with honesty, show the label.

## 3. Honest weaknesses (say them, or design around them)
| Weakness (Stated, §2.7 unless noted) | What it means for creative |
|---|---|
| **Her shedding may be telogen effluvium** (the mainstream view: a temporary shed after fast weight loss), not DHT | Never "the real cause is DHT". Agree with nutrition first; the estrogen story is "one reason" |
| **No finished-product trial**; the pumpkin-seed-oil study was on **men** and on the ingredient | No "clinically proven", no study numbers in ads. "Studied for how they work on DHT" at most |
| **About 3 months to see results** vs her "make it stop now" | Set the timeline in every ad; never promise "within weeks" |
| **The trigger keeps firing while she keeps losing / steps up a dose** | Don't promise a hard stop; "supports" language; this is also why the dose-increase concept must not imply we stop the restart |
| **Single mechanism** for a multi-cause problem | Honest disqualifier (A27) is a page/variation test, not a hook |
| **Not vegan** (gelatin softgel) | FAQ/page; not an ad issue unless comments raise it |
| **No GLP-1 testimonials** | No customer-result lines at all; AI characters never claim results |
| **"Is it safe with my shot?"** can't be answered by us | Only "check with your doctor about your routine"; never imply compatibility (hard ban #1). "Companion" wording can raise this worry (open question) |
| **"What happens if I stop?"**: no honest answer yet (`failed-solutions.md`) | Don't claim "no dependency" until there is one |
| Default offer = 1 month (§1.3) | Landing page should nudge 3 months; ads say "about three months" |

## 4. What the landing page must carry (because the ads can't)
The research's funnel for GLP-1 is cold → **advertorial** → PDP (§1.4): the mechanism needs room. **Whether a GLP-1
advertorial or landing page exists is not known** (`running-notes/missing-context.md`). If round-1 ads send her to the
menopause page, the ad and page won't match and CVR will read low for the wrong reason.
