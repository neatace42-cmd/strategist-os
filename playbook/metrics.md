# Metrics: what they mean and what a weak one tells you

## The metrics
| Metric | Formula | What it shows | Starting benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook rate** | 3-second views ÷ impressions | Did the first seconds stop the scroll? | 30%+ (great: 45–50%) |
| **Hold rate** | ThruPlays ÷ 3-second views | Did she keep watching after the hook? | 12–15%+ |
| **CTR (outbound)** | Link clicks ÷ impressions | Did the ad make her want to know more? | about 1%+ |
| **CPM** | Spend ÷ impressions × 1,000 | Cost to reach people (auction + audience) | compare to our own trend |
| **CPA** | Spend ÷ purchases | Cost per customer: the money truth | below our max CPA *(owner-held)* |
| **ROAS** | Revenue ÷ spend | Return on spend | above our break-even *(owner-held)* |
| **Frequency** | Impressions ÷ reach | How often the same person saw it | above 1.5–2 = fatigue warning |
| **Conversion rate** | Purchases ÷ link clicks | Did the page close the sale? | compare to our own trend |

## Rules for reading results
- **Spend × ROAS is the truth.** A 5× ROAS on $30 is not a winner; a steady 2× on $5,000 might be.
- Never kill a profitable ad just because CPA is slightly high.
- **Fatigue needs a trend, not one bad day.** One warning sign is a hint, two is a pattern, three is fatigue.
  Signs: frequency rising past 1.5–2, ROAS below its lifetime level, hook or hold rate falling, CPM rising while reach
  stays flat, CPA rising.
- Judge hooks on **spend plus hook rate**, never ROAS alone.
- Exact numbers in reports, with their base. No rounding into "about".

## What a weak metric points to (where to iterate)
| What you see | Likely problem | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| Low hook rate | The first 1–3 s don't stop her | New opening visual or sound, new first line, new hook type |
| Good hook, low hold | Pacing, or the body drifts | Tighten pacing, show the problem sooner, re-hook at 3–5 s |
| Low CTR, but buyers convert well | Not enough people click | Hook, headline, visual. Protect the body |
| High CTR, low conversion | The ad promises what the page doesn't deliver | Match ad and landing page; check the offer |
| High completion, low CTR | The ending doesn't move her | Stronger CTA or offer |
| CPA and frequency both rising | Fatigue | New creator, new format, or a new angle |
| Good numbers but won't scale | The audience is used up | New format, new creator type, different awareness level |

## Which change is "louder" (biggest effect first)
Format › vehicle (the story device) › persona › hook › messaging line.
Order of what to change on a fading winner: **angle first, then format, then persona last.**
One variable per iteration. Default: 3 iterations. Never iterate on a loser.
