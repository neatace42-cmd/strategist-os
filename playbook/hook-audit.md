# Monthly hook audit

**Goal:** know which openings work for us, and find new ones before the current ones tire.

1. **Our hooks:** from the last 4 weekly reports, list every video ad's hook (the `H#` in the ad name and what it
   was). Judge on **spend + hook rate together**, never ROAS alone.
   - Strong: hook rate ≥ 30% at meaningful spend.
   - "Weak" needs a reason: truly weak · tired (it used to work; frequency up) · good hook, the body loses her later
     (hold rate low) · not enough data.
2. **Outside hooks:** 5–10 videos each from competitors (`competitors/hooks-swipe.md`), organic TikTok in the
   GLP-1 / hair space (Trendtrack TikTok library, modest), and one far-away category. Video only, no promo ads.
3. **Write** `reports/hook-audit-YYYY-MM.md`: our top 5 and bottom 5 with the reason, 10 new hook ideas (picture +
   sound + words) tagged safe bet / untapped / iteration, each saved as an idea-bank entry.
