# The method, in plain English

How this workspace turns research into ads worth testing. Written in my own words; for deeper study see
`learning-path.md`.

## 1. The building blocks (keep them separate)
| Block | Question it answers | NutraEssence example |
|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | What broad problem? | Hair shedding on a weight-loss journey |
| **Angle** | Why should she care, right now? (the reason) | "You did the protein and it's still falling out, because it was never a nutrition problem" |
| **Persona** | Who, and what triggered her? (named by trigger, not age) | "I did everything right and it's STILL falling out" |
| **Overlay** | A life stage or situation that sits on top of several personas | Perimenopause, dose increase, diabetic |
| **Doorway** | How she enters | *Switch* (leave an old fix: biotin, Nutrafol) · *Awakening* (a cause she didn't know: the estrogen shield) · *Discovery* (already looking) |
| **Format** | The container | UGC testimonial, authority explainer, static, listicle |
| **Hook** | The first 1–3 seconds (picture + sound + words) | The hair tie that now wraps four times |

A new hook is **not** a new angle. A new format is **not** a new angle. Changing the angle means changing the reason.

## 2. Awareness and sophistication
- **Awareness (Schwartz's 5 levels):** unaware → problem-aware → solution-aware → product-aware → most-aware.
  Research estimate for our cold traffic: about **50% problem-aware**, 25% solution-aware (§0.3, *Stated*,
  directional). So most cold ads should open on her problem or its real cause, not on the product.
- **Sophistication:** how many claims she has already heard. She has tried 3+ fixes (protein, biotin, often Nutrafol or
  minoxidil), so **high**: acknowledge what failed and give a sharper *mechanism* (why this works when they didn't).
- Keep a healthy share (roughly half) of new concepts aimed at unaware/problem-aware buyers; too much bottom-funnel
  creative stalls growth.

## 3. Where angles come from (in this order)
1. **Customer words**: reviews, comments, Reddit. Her phrases are the messaging.
2. **A format to carry it**: borrowed from organic content and brands with an overlapping audience.
3. **Our own winners**, and our losers (what not to repeat).
4. **Competitors**, last (where they crowd one claim is where not to lead).
Also useful: purchase **triggers** (the moment she buys today: the clump in the shower, the photo, the dose increase)
are different from **pains** (which she can carry for months).

## 4. Every concept carries one hypothesis
> We believe **[persona]** at **[awareness level]** will respond to **[angle]** because **[insight + its source]**.
> We'll know if **[metric]** beats **[benchmark]** within **[spend or days]**.
Write down what stays fixed and what would prove it wrong. One idea, one test.

## 5. Judging ideas: evidence bands
- **HIGH**: our own winner, or her exact words seen across several sources.
- **MEDIUM**: one strong source (a competitor ad running for months, one clear review cluster).
- **LOW**: a hunch or one thin source.
Rank by **confidence first, then speed to ship**. Cut ideas that clash with the roadmap's "roads not taken".

## 6. From idea to concept to brief
- An **idea** is a spark. A **concept** is the idea adapted to our brand with **2–4 variations** that look clearly
  different (hook, setting, length), plus a static version when it makes sense.
- **Round size:** look at how many truly new (non-sale) concepts go live per week now; plan about double that, limited
  by budget. Most concepts go to the current priority; protect a share for proven winners.
- **Testing mix:** about 70% iterations on winners, 20% new angles, 10% wild experiments (owner's working rule).

## 7. For our buyer (women 40+)
Clear beats clever. Stay in the problem a little longer; she follows longer stories. Show women her own age. Authority
and plain explanation build trust. Big readable text, calmer cuts (every 3–5 s). Calm "permission" openers ("You don't
have to choose…") beat loud fear on cold traffic. Supplement ads are **scripted**, not freestyled, because of claims.
