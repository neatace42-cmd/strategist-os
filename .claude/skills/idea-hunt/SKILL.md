# Idea hunt (weekly) and evaluation

**Capture (no judging yet)**
1. Write a 5-line hunt brief first: roadmap priorities, what's working, what failed, what the bank is short of.
2. Cold brainstorm 5 ideas BEFORE opening any feed.
3. Hunt in this order: her words (`personas/voice-of-customer/`, new reviews), organic content (Trendtrack TikTok
   library, modest credits, logged), our old winners, one far-away category, competitors last.
4. One file per idea in `idea-bank/entries/` using `idea-bank/_entry-template.md`; add it to `idea-bank/index.md`.

**Evaluate**
5. For each untested idea: which roadmap priority and persona it serves, which lever (persona / product / message /
   creator), evidence band (HIGH: own winner or her words across sources · MEDIUM: one strong source · LOW: thin).
6. Cut ideas that hit the roadmap's "roads not taken". Rank by confidence, then speed to ship.
7. Write `idea-bank/evaluation-YYYY-MM-DD.md`, including "what the bank is short of". Log the routine.
