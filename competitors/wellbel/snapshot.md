## Claims we must NOT copy (compliance)
- An "MD" face in our ads would need a real, paid, disclosed doctor. We have none. Never make up a doctor or "expert". (claims-guide)
- "GLP-1 side effects" as a hook topic is close to a drug-side-effect claim. Keep ours on "what women on a weight-loss journey notice". (Inferred)
- Their wording is the **safe model**: "nutritional support for healthy hair from within", "can actually play a role in supporting you". This is structure/function language we **can** learn from. (Verified; Inferred lesson)

## Where they are weak / what they leave open for us
- **They have saw palmetto but tell a nutrition story.** They never connect "less body fat → less estrogen → DHT matters more". The research saw this gap in August and it is still open. (Stated §7.2; Verified in 1 transcript, small base)
- No pumpkin seed oil, and doses are not in the ads. (Stated)
- Small brand, little visible proof. (Stated)
- Very short ads (about 30 s): little room to explain a mechanism. A longer "why" ad has room. (Inferred)
