## The biggest real competitor: "do nothing / wait it out"
- Most women just put up with it: wait and see, cut it short, wear a topper, or "it's just the price of being thin". (Stated, §7.1)
- It is **partly fair**: pure telogen effluvium (stress shedding) often settles once weight is stable. Concede that honestly. (Stated, §6.3)
- Her own words: "wait and see… I hate that" (research §5.4, quoted in §6.3). Waiting has a cost if a slower, hormone-driven thinning is being "unmasked" while the shed keeps going. Say it as education, not as a scare or a diagnosis. (Stated §6.3; claims-guide caveat: DHT is education, not a proven cause)
- Inferred: every rival above is fighting each other for the woman who already decided to act. The bigger pool is the woman who has not decided yet, and **"you don't have to wait and hope"** is a door few rivals open (MDhair touches it with "Every week you wait, those follicles get weaker", in a fear-based way).

## Working thesis (Inferred)
**Crowded, don't lead there:** "GLP-1 hair loss is real / your hair is last in line for nutrients / you don't have to choose between results and hair". MDhair, Wellbel, Nutrafol, Moérie, IM8 and Nutrition Geeks all say some version of it, so on its own it is invisible. **Open, lead there:** the honest *why* (body fat makes estrogen; less of it and DHT matters more), told without fake studies, for the woman who already fixed her protein and is still shedding. But move fast, because Bloom & Bond started telling exactly this story 8 days ago with louder claims.
