# MDhair: snapshot

**Labels:** Stated = the research says so · Verified = seen in Trendtrack/Atria data today · Inferred = my reasoning · Data-limited = too little data to be sure.

## Threat type
**Biggest active spender in GLP-1 hair right now.** It reframes the problem ("it's not the drug, it's your nutrients"), and its quiz funnel makes it look personal and medical. (Verified volume, Trendtrack; Stated framing, §7.2)

## What they sell, price, guarantee
- A personalised hair kit from a quiz + scalp photo: serum, supplements, collagen, shampoo, conditioner. Often includes compounded minoxidil. (Stated, §7.2) The ads call one version "non-medicated". (Verified, transcript)
- Offer in the ads: **"60% off"** the first kit. (Verified, several transcripts)
- Guarantee: **"they have a 180 day guarantee"** (spoken in one creator ad, Trendtrack). (Verified that the ad says it; the actual terms are not checked)
- Price: not in the data. (Data-limited)

## Who their ads target
Base: the 20 longest-running active ads Trendtrack returned (of 146 active ads in the brand search; the page shows 448 live ads).
- **12 of 20** target women (ages 24–65), **7 of 20** men (21–65), 1 not stated. (Verified)
- **7 of 20** send traffic to GLP-1 pages: listicles "10 things you know about GLP-1 hair loss (women)" and "9 reasons MDhair is the better approach for GLP-1 hair loss". (Verified)
- Women personas seen: the woman on GLP-1 who lost a lot of weight ("losing 90 pounds"), the perimenopausal woman ("I'm 49 years old"), the frustrated product-hopper ("I've tried like five different shampoos this year"). (Verified, transcripts)
- Mostly UK audiences (main country GB on every row), also US/CA/AU. (Verified)

## Main angles and messages
1. **"It's not the drug, it's the change / your nutrients"** (myth-busting). "GLP-1 is permanently damaging your follicles? False. It's not the medication attacking your hair; it's the rapid change your body's going through." (spoken, Trendtrack, 53 days, about 89k reach, ranked 9th on their page) (Verified)
2. **"Your hair is last in line"** (nutrient priority). "Your heart, your brain, your gut, your hair follicles are last in line. They get nothing." (spoken, "hair loss specialist" ad, 68 days) (Verified)
3. **"You don't have to choose"** (keep the drug, keep the hair). "You don't have to trade your hair for your results." (spoken, 53 days) (Verified)
4. **"Generic products fail you, find your cause"** (personalisation). "Generic solutions will produce generic results for most women dealing with hair loss." (spoken, 191 days) and "Three types of women losing their hair." (spoken, 140 days) (Verified)
5. **DHT for men**, nutrition for GLP-1 women. Men's ads: "After 35, a hormone called DHT starts attacking your follicles directly" (spoken, 363k reach). Women's GLP-1 ads barely mention DHT. (Verified) This matches the research: "DHT blockers" are added on top of a nutrition-first story. (Stated, §7.2)

## Claims we must NOT copy (compliance)
- "If you're on Ozempic or Wagovi, and your hair is falling out, you need to hear this." Drug brand names + states the viewer's condition (Meta personal attributes). (Verified ad; Inferred risk)
- "A first-of-its-kind, clinically proven, customized hair regrowth treatment" (ad body on every ad): "first", "clinically proven", "regrowth treatment" = 3 hard bans for us. (Verified)
- Customer results in numbers ("Within a week of switching to MD Hair, there was considerably less hair falling out"). We have no GLP-1 testimonials yet, so we cannot write anything like this. (claims-guide)
- "Clinical results can show in as little as one month." (Verified ad; we have no product study)
- Fear-of-loss lines like "Every week you wait, those follicles get weaker." Fine as a feeling, but do not make a medical prediction. (Inferred)

## Where they are weak / what they leave open for us
- **They say the medication is not the cause and it is all nutrition.** That makes "eat more protein / take vitamins" the answer, and she already tried that (§6.3). The hormonal "why" for women (estrogen falls with fat loss → DHT matters more) is **not in their women's ads**. (Verified in the sample; Inferred opportunity)
- Their kit is a big routine (5 products + quiz + app). We are 2 softgels. Simplicity is a counter. (Inferred)
- Minoxidil in many kits = the "dread shed" and "forever on it" fear. (Stated, §7.2)
- They lean on a UK audience; US share is unclear. (Data-limited)
