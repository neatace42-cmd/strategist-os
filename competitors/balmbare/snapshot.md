# Balmbare: snapshot

**Labels:** Stated = the research says so · Verified = seen in Trendtrack/Atria data today · Inferred = my reasoning · Data-limited = too little data to be sure.

## Threat type
**Narrative thief.** They told our exact story first (GLP-1 → hormones → DHT), but with a weaker formula and hype-style proof. (Stated, research §7.2)

## What they sell, price, guarantee
- **Hair Revive** gummies (also capsules, oil, serum; "Revive Plus" bundles). Actives: nettle (their DHT blocker), pearl powder, Blue Majik, sea moss, aloe, biotin, vitamins C/E. **No saw palmetto, no pumpkin seed oil.** (Stated, §7.2)
- Price about $56 a bottle, about $40 in bundles, about $2/day. (Stated, §7.2)
- **60-day** money-back guarantee. (Stated, §7.2; Verified in their current joint-product ads: "try it with a sixty day money back guarantee", Trendtrack)
- New: the same company now sells a **joint collagen** line ("Revive Mix" / "More Than Collagen" under the name **BALMVE**, balmve.com), subscription only, "Buy 2, Get 2 FREE", $69. (Verified, Trendtrack)

## Who their ads target
- Research: women on GLP-1 medication who are problem/solution aware. (Stated, §7.2)
- Today's active ads target **women with stiff knees and joints**, for example "keep up with the grandkids". (Verified, Trendtrack transcripts)
- The GLP-1 hair ads we found ran through a creator page called **"Elizabeth Moore"** (whitelisted creator, meaning the brand pays to run ads under a person's page). (Verified, Atria)

## Main angles and messages
**Big change since the research: hair is no longer what Balmbare is pushing on Meta.**
- **19 of 19** active Balmbare ads that Trendtrack returned (brand search, sorted by run length) are joint/collagen ads. **0 of 19** are about hair or GLP-1. (Verified, Trendtrack) Their pages show 160 and 420 live ads, so this sample is small. (Data-limited)
- Atria found **3** Balmbare GLP-1 hair ads, and **all 3 are inactive**. They ran 13, 25 and 28 days (Aug 2025, Mar–Apr 2026, May–Jun 2026). (Verified, Atria)
- Angles in those hair ads:
  - **"Everything else failed"** (the graveyard of tried products): "You tried biotin, collagen, maybe even Nutrafol for your GLP-1 hair loss… None of them worked." (on-screen/ad copy, Atria, 28 days, inactive)
  - **Tips / how-to**: "Here's what to do to stop the shedding when you're on a GLP-1." (ad copy, Atria, 25 days, inactive)
  - **Social proof**: "Over 55,000 women trust BalmBare." (ad copy, Atria)
  - **Symptoms vs cause**, the research's hero hook: "Every solution you've tried treats symptoms. None address what GLP-1 medications actually do to your hormones." (Stated, §7.2; not seen in today's data)
- Joint ads (for format learning only): **talking-body-part animation** ("I'm your knee cartilage… I'm starving"), **price objection answered** ("Why is Revive Mix more expensive than normal collagen?"), **comment reply** ("Minda asked why Bonvi doesn't do one-time orders" — the speech-to-text writes BALMVE as "Bonvi"). (Verified, Trendtrack transcripts)

## Formats and what seems to be scaling
- Research: advertorials plus shower-clump UGC (user-made style video) and affiliate funnels. (Stated, §7.3)
- Seen today: all 19 sampled active ads are videos; up to 6 copies of one creative. Reach on all of them is tiny (1 to 118 per ad in Trendtrack), so **none look like they are scaling**. (Verified, Trendtrack; reach for US-only ads may be under-counted, Data-limited)
- Inferred: short hair-ad runs (13–28 days) plus the switch to joints suggest the GLP-1 hair push did not keep paying, or moved to channels we cannot see (advertorials, affiliates, TikTok). (Inferred)

## Claims we must NOT copy (compliance)
- "The FIRST/ONLY supplement created specifically for GLP-1 hair loss" — hard ban #4. (Stated, §7.2)
- "Stop GLP-1 Hair Loss →" (ad title) and "stop the shedding" — treatment of a drug side effect, hard ban #2. (Verified, Atria)
- "Bald spots? Thinning edges? Weak strands? Not anymore" — a cure promise. (Verified, Atria ad copy, Aug 2025)
- "Buried 2024 NIH study", "Dr. Amanda Richardson", "4.5× more effective", "up to 38% DHT reduction in 8 weeks" — proof that sounds made up. (Stated, §7.2 and the strategic update)
- "Losing hair on GLP-1?"-style questions that state the viewer's medication — Meta personal-attribute rule.

## Where they are weak / what they leave open for us
- Weaker DHT ingredient (nettle) and gummy doses buried in sugar. We have 2,000 mg pumpkin seed oil + 200 mg saw palmetto, doses shown on the label. (Stated, §7.2)
- Trust: their own "is Balmbare a scam?" page exists. Proof that sounds fake is their weak spot; **honest proof is ours.** (Stated, §7.2)
- 60-day guarantee vs our 90. (Stated)
- **They seem to have stepped back from GLP-1 hair on Meta**, which leaves the hormonal story less crowded than the research thought. But a new rival is now running it (see `_competitive-set.md`, Bloom & Bond). (Verified for Balmbare's active sample; Inferred for the meaning)
