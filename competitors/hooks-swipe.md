## Patterns across the 20 (Inferred)
- **13 of 20** are plain problem-aware hooks (3 more bridge into problem-aware, 4 are solution-aware). Almost nobody speaks to the *unaware* woman (just started, not shedding yet) or the *product-aware* one.
- **Permission / "you don't have to choose"** (#10, #11, MDhair's "you don't have to trade your hair for your results") is turning into the category's default line.
- **Guilt removal** ("you're not eating wrong") + **a new cause** (#17) is the strongest structure here, and the one closest to our mechanism.
- Long-running hooks (#4, #5: 140–191 days) are **format-led** (types skit, step list), not claim-led. That is a hint for our format tests.
