and is still shedding. Our opening is the honest "why": agree that weight loss plays a part, then explain the hormone
layer underneath, with the doses on the label. Bloom & Bond started a louder version of this 8 days ago, so speed matters.

**The 3 priorities**
1. **P1, the honest why**: "you weren't doing it wrong; here's the part underneath." Strongest evidence.
2. **P2, permission with a reason**: "wanting your hair back isn't vanity", plus the dose step-up. Real ache, unproven urgency.
3. **P6, don't wait and hope**: one cheap test for the woman who hasn't started shedding. Weakest evidence.
